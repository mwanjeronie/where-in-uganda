Where in Uganda? Find your village, parish, sub-county and district

Filling in a NIN or NIRA registration, a KCCA document, or a school form? Search the place you know and read off the village, parish, sub-county, county and district that belong with it.

How to find the names for your form

  1. Type a village, parish, town or district into the search box.
  2. Open a result. For a village, the path reads district, county, sub-county, parish, then the village.
  3. Copy the parish, sub-county and district onto your NIN, NIRA, KCCA or school form. If you are unsure of the spelling, tap Use my location and check the places listed near you.

Common questions

How do I find my parish for NIN registration?

Search for your village name and open the result that matches. The path shown with a village runs district, county, sub-county, parish, then village. The parish is the name immediately before the village. Use that parish, with the sub-county and district, on the NIN or NIRA form.

What is the difference between a county and a sub-county?

A county is the larger area inside a district. A sub-county sits inside a county, and parishes sit inside the sub-county. On every village in this search the path is district, county, sub-county, parish, then village, so the county is the second name and the sub-county is the third.

Is it free?

Yes. Searching for a village, parish, sub-county, county or district is free.

How many locations are covered?

The search covers more than 84,000 locations, including villages, parishes, sub-counties, counties and districts.

Can I use this for KCCA and school forms?

Yes. The village, parish, sub-county and district on a result are the names people copy onto KCCA documents and school forms, as well as NIN and NIRA forms.

What if the same place name appears more than once?

Open each result and compare the path: district, county, sub-county and parish. Pick the one that matches the area you mean. You can also filter the search to parish, sub-county, county, district or village.
